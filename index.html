Dear Papa,

Happy Teachers Day!

Today, through this little website, I want to wish the happiest Teachers’ Day to the person who has been my very first teacher, my greatest mentor, and one of the most precious blessings in my life — my father. You were the first person who taught me how to speak, how to walk, how to understand the world, and how to find my way through it. Long before I learned anything from books or classrooms, I learned from you. Whatever I am today, whatever I have learned, and whatever skills I carry in my hands — even the ability to create this website, to communicate with people, and to express myself — is a reflection of your effort, your guidance, your patience, and your prayers.

I truly believe that a person can have many teachers throughout life, but there can hardly be a teacher greater than a parent. And for me, no teacher could ever hold a place higher than you. On this Teachers’ Day, I pray that Allah Almighty blesses you with every success and every happiness of this world and the Hereafter. May He fulfill your heartfelt wishes, fill your heart with everlasting peace, and keep you steadfast upon Islam throughout your life. May Allah grant you a life filled with faith, goodness, dignity, and countless blessings. And when the time comes for Allah to call you back to Him, may He grant you the honour of meeting Him in a state of complete faith and with a heart full of Imaan.

And may Allah, in Jannah, bless you with the honour of meeting and being in the company of our beloved Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. May Allah accept every prayer you have ever made, reward every sacrifice you have ever made for me, and make every effort you have invested in me a source of endless reward for you. Ameen, Ya Rabb-ul-Alameen. Happy Teachers’ Day, Papa. ❤️

With endless love & respect,

Your Loving Child ❤️
